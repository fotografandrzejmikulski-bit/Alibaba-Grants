## One-sentence proposition
OMEGA-X is an agentic AI migration-qualification and conversion engine that turns approved AWS infrastructure information into a validated Alibaba Cloud migration plan, evidence package, and bounded pilot workflow.

## Why Alibaba Cloud should care
OMEGA-X is designed to reduce the technical friction that slows enterprise cloud-switching decisions. Its intended commercial role is not to replace Alibaba Cloud sales or solution engineering, but to make enterprise opportunities easier to qualify, demonstrate, and convert.

## Current evidence
The project brief identifies **Alpha 0.4** and reports successful scanning of basic Terraform descriptors with translation toward Alibaba Cloud equivalents in local containerized environments. These are source-stated capabilities, not externally audited claims.

## What the program enables
1. Move the existing local workflow into controlled Alibaba Cloud-hosted validation.
2. Measure baseline versus OMEGA-X migration-qualification effort.
3. Validate security, policy, artifact integrity, and bounded-agent controls.
4. Execute a structured 15-pilot Enterprise Pilot Program.
5. Attribute technical outcomes and Alibaba Cloud consumption to successful migrations.

## Core AI workload
A coordinator agent routes bounded tasks to Architect, Developer, and Security agents. Model Studio/Qwen-family models are proposed for reasoning, code transformation, architecture interpretation, and validation assistance. Model output remains untrusted until deterministic validation and policy gates pass.

## Primary KPIs
- migration qualification cycle time,
- manual engineering hours avoided,
- first-pass IaC validation rate,
- generated-artifact defect rate,
- POC-to-pilot conversion,
- pilot-to-production conversion,
- AI inference/context efficiency per migration,
- attributable Alibaba Cloud workload consumption.

## Important positioning discipline
The proposal treats 60% cycle-time reduction, 70% audit-effort reduction, and 40% AI-cost reduction as **targets to validate**, not historical results. It treats public funding/token maximums as **program ceilings subject to approval and current terms**, not guaranteed entitlements.

## Decision request
Support a controlled technical validation and pilot phase for OMEGA-X under the applicable Alibaba Cloud AI Catalyst terms, with success judged by measured enterprise migration outcomes rather than unsupported commercial guarantees.
