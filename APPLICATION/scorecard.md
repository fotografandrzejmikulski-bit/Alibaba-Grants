# OMEGA-X — Internal Reviewer Scorecard

Use this scorecard before submission and again after any major product or evidence update.

| Dimension | Weight | Current assessment | Evidence required for top score |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Problem clarity | 15% | Strong | quantified baseline from pilots |
| Alibaba Cloud strategic fit | 20% | Strong | attributable opportunity/pilot evidence |
| AI differentiation | 15% | Strong | reproducible multi-agent workflow and benchmark |
| Technical feasibility | 15% | Strong | hosted demo + repeatable fixture |
| Security/governance | 10% | Strong | executable controls and test results |
| Traction/readiness | 10% | Developing | reproducible Alpha 0.4 evidence; pilot pipeline |
| Budget justification | 10% | Strong conceptually | actual service estimates and pilot attribution |
| Applicant credibility | 5% | Developing | verified profile/deck/project evidence |

## Hard-fail checks

- No invented company, customer, revenue, grant award, GPU availability, or legal-compliance claim.
- No private credential or account identifier committed to the public repository.
- No production deployment path that bypasses deterministic validation or human approval.
- No claim of completed pilots until evidence exists.

## Evidence maturity

**E0 — assertion:** claim stated but not evidenced.  
**E1 — artifact:** file, fixture, screenshot, or reproducible output exists.  
**E2 — repeatable:** independent rerun produces the expected result.  
**E3 — pilot-validated:** measured on real or representative pilot workloads.  
**E4 — independently corroborated:** external/customer/partner evidence confirms outcome.

The grant submission should maximize E1/E2 for current capabilities and clearly label E3/E4 work as future validation where it does not yet exist.
